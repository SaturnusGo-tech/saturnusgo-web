Falcon for Mac

Falcon for macOS

Falcon, at home on your Mac.

Install once. Open from Applications. Your workspace is ready when you are.

Move Falcon to Applications

Open the downloaded DMG and drag Falcon onto the Applications folder. Wait for the copy to finish, then eject the disk image. Open Falcon from Applications, not from the DMG.

Open Falcon for the first time

Try opening Falcon from Applications. If macOS says it cannot verify the developer or check the app, dismiss that message and follow the next step. If Falcon opens normally, you are done.

Allow this app in Privacy & Security

Open Apple menu → System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll to Security. Next to the message about Falcon being blocked, choose Open Anyway. Confirm the next dialog and authenticate with Touch ID or your Mac password if asked.

Open Anyway appears after an attempted launch. If you cannot see it, try opening Falcon again, then return to Privacy & Security. On a company-managed Mac, your administrator may control this permission.

About this beta

This build is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple. The beta label is not what triggers the warning. Only allow a copy you downloaded from Falcon and trust. This exception applies to Falcon; you do not need to disable Gatekeeper. If macOS reports malware or a damaged app, stop and contact support instead.
